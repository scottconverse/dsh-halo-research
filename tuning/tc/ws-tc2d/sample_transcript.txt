CITY COMMISSION REGULAR MEETING
August 10, 2026 - City Hall, Council Chambers

CHAIRWOMAN ALVAREZ: We will come to order at 7:02 p.m. The public comment period is open for five minutes. Tonight's agenda covers the FY2027 budget and a zoning variance request on Maple Street.

COMMISSIONER CHEN: Let us take up the FY2027 budget first. The proposed general fund totals $48.2 million, an increase of 3 percent over last year. Public safety receives the largest line-item increase at $1.9 million, and the library receives a flat appropriation of $6.4 million.

COMMISSIONER OKAFOR: Did the projections receive independent review?

CITY MANAGER PATEL: Yes. The city's audit firm reviewed the revenue projections in June and confirmed them.

COMMISSIONER CHEN: I move to adopt the FY2027 budget as presented.

COMMISSIONER OKAFOR: Seconded.

CHAIRWOMAN ALVAREZ: All those in favor say aye.

CHEN, OKAFOR, RAMIREZ, WHITFIELD: Aye.

COMMISSIONER DIAZ: Nay.

CHAIRWOMAN ALVAREZ: The motion carries 4 to 1. The FY2027 budget is adopted at $48.2 million.

CHAIRWOMAN ALVAREZ: We will now take up the zoning variance request for 412 Maple Street, which seeks a reduced front setback from 25 feet to 18 feet.

COMMISSIONER RAMIREZ: The planning staff report recommends denial because the lot does not meet the hardship standard.

COMMISSIONER WHITFIELD: I support the staff recommendation.

CITY MANAGER PATEL: The applicant's attorney states that the slope of the property makes the 25-foot setback impracticable.

CHAIRWOMAN ALVAREZ: On the motion to deny the variance, all in favor say aye.

ALVAREZ, CHEN, RAMIREZ, WHITFIELD: Aye.

COMMISSIONER OKAFOR: Nay.
COMMISSIONER DIAZ: Nay.

CHAIRWOMAN ALVAREZ: The motion carries 4 to 2. The variance is denied. This meeting is adjourned at 7:41 p.m.
